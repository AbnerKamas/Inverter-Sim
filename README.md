# Inverter-Sim

Simulation and design of high-frequency inverter circuits, built and tested in **LTspice**, as part of a power electronics optimization project.

## Overview

This project explores the design and simulation of inverter circuits for high-frequency power conversion applications. The goal is to improve energy efficiency and control flexibility in switching-based inverter topologies, evaluating how circuit parameters affect performance under varying load and switching conditions.

Key areas of focus include:

- Designing inverter topologies suited to high-frequency switching
- Simulating circuit behavior in LTspice to evaluate efficiency, ripple, and thermal characteristics
- Iterating on component selection and control strategy to improve overall energy efficiency
- Exploring trade-offs between switching frequency, losses, and output quality

## Tools

- **LTspice** — circuit design, simulation, and waveform analysis

## Repository Contents

- Circuit schematics (`.asc` files)
- Simulation results and waveform plots
- Notes on design iterations and parameter choices

## Background

This work builds on coursework and independent study in power electronics, with the broader aim of applying simulation-driven design to practical energy systems.
